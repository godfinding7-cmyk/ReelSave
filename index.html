Questions

Do I need to log in?

No. Paste the link of a public post. No Instagram account or app is needed.

Can I download from a private account?

No. Private posts can't be accessed, and we never ask for your Instagram password.

Where is the video saved?

On a computer it goes to your Downloads folder. On Android check Downloads or Files. On iPhone use Safari, tap the download icon, then open Files.

Why does it say the post could not be fetched?

The post may be private or deleted, or Instagram may be limiting requests right now. Try again in a few minutes.

Is it free?

Yes, and there is no sign-up. To keep the service fast, each device can make a limited number of requests per minute.
